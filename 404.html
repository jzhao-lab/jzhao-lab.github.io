About Me

I am Junyi Zhao, a Postdoctoral Scholar at Stanford University, working with Prof. Zhenan Bao. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Washington University in St. Louis (WashU) and previously worked as a Research Scientist Intern at Meta Reality Labs.

My research focuses on human-centered soft electronics for sensing, perception, and feedback, spanning innovations in materials, devices, advanced manufacturing, system integrations, multimodal data analytics, and translational deployment.

I will join the University of Wisconsin–Madison in Fall 2027 as a tenure-track Assistant Professor, with a joint appointment in the Department of Mechanical Engineering (ME) and the Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering (ECE). Join us
